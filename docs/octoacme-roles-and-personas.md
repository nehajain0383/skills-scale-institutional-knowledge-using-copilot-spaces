# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Managers:** Clarify requirements and acceptance criteria
- **With Project Managers:** Report progress, risks, and blockers
- **With QA Lead:** Review test plans and provide build deliverables
- **With DevOps Specialist:** Discuss deployment concerns and infrastructure requirements

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers:** Define features and validate solutions
- **With Project Managers:** Align on timeline and scope
- **With Scrum Master:** Plan sprints and refine backlog
- **With Stakeholder Representatives:** Gather requirements and feedback

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers:** Track progress and manage blockers
- **With Product Managers:** Ensure alignment on priorities and scope
- **With QA Lead:** Coordinate testing timelines and quality gates
- **With DevOps Specialist:** Plan deployment windows and infrastructure needs

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
The QA Lead ensures that delivered software meets agreed quality standards and user expectations. They design test strategies, manage defect reporting, and collaborate with developers and stakeholders to verify acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategies and quality acceptance criteria
- Create and maintain test plans and test cases
- Execute testing activities (manual and automated)
- Identify, document, and track defects
- Collaborate with developers to resolve quality issues
- Report on quality metrics and test coverage

### Goals
- Ensure all deliverables meet quality standards before production
- Reduce defects in production through proactive testing
- Improve test efficiency and coverage over time
- Enable rapid, confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews with development team
- Defect reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Status updates in standup meetings and sprint reviews
- Feedback sessions with stakeholders on quality findings

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers:** Review build deliverables, discuss edge cases, and validate fixes
- **With Project Managers:** Coordinate testing timelines and quality gates
- **With DevOps Specialist:** Execute integration and deployment testing
- **With Product Managers:** Validate acceptance criteria and user scenarios

---

## DevOps Specialist

### Role Summary
The DevOps Specialist automates and streamlines deployment processes, manages CI/CD pipelines, and bridges development and operations. They ensure infrastructure reliability and enable rapid, safe releases.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Automate deployment and infrastructure provisioning
- Monitor system performance, availability, and security
- Manage configuration management and environment consistency
- Support incident response and troubleshooting
- Collaborate with developers on build and deployment concerns

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and repeatable deployments
- Minimize deployment failures and production incidents
- Reduce time-to-production for new features
- Maintain secure, scalable infrastructure

### Typical Communication
- Deployment readiness reviews with development and QA
- Infrastructure documentation and runbooks
- Incident post-mortems and lessons learned
- Collaboration during release planning

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers:** Support build integration and deployment concerns
- **With QA Lead:** Execute integration and deployment testing
- **With Project Managers:** Coordinate release windows and deployment readiness
- **With Scrum Master:** Report deployment metrics and infrastructure blockers

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master facilitates agile ceremonies, removes impediments, and fosters a high-performing team culture. They ensure the team adheres to agile methodologies and continuously improves delivery practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and blockers for the team
- Coach the team on agile principles and practices
- Maintain sprint board and track velocity
- Identify and drive process improvements
- Foster psychological safety and team collaboration

### Goals
- Enable the team to deliver consistently high-quality work
- Maximize team productivity and throughput
- Continuously improve team processes and practices
- Foster a culture of accountability and transparency

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation and blocker tracking
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- One-on-ones to support team members

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Managers:** Support backlog refinement and sprint planning
- **With Developers:** Remove blockers and support team dynamics
- **With Project Managers:** Coordinate timeline and scope discussions
- **With QA Lead:** Ensure quality standards are built into sprint definition of done

---

## Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Representatives bring external perspectives to the project, voice stakeholder needs and concerns, and ensure that project requirements align with business objectives. They serve as a bridge between the project team and broader organizational interests.

### Responsibilities
- Represent stakeholder needs and priorities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment
- Review project milestones and deliverables
- Communicate project status to broader stakeholder groups
- Provide feedback on prototypes and solutions
- Escalate concerns or misalignments

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and meet stakeholder expectations
- Maintain alignment between project delivery and strategic objectives
- Reduce scope creep and prioritize high-impact features
- Foster transparency and trust with stakeholder community

### Typical Communication
- Milestone review meetings and status briefings
- Feature demos and feedback sessions
- Stakeholder updates and executive summaries
- Requirements clarification and prioritization discussions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Managers:** Provide stakeholder feedback and business priorities
- **With Project Managers:** Participate in planning and status reporting
- **With Developers:** Attend demos and provide user perspective feedback
- **With QA Lead:** Review quality standards and acceptance criteria

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Reference persona interactions to understand cross-functional collaboration points and communication patterns.
- Consider role responsibilities when assigning tasks and defining handoff criteria in project management processes.
- Use the RACI matrix in `octoacme-raci-matrix.md` to clarify accountability and decision-making authority for specific activities.
